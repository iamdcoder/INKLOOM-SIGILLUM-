## 1. Build-window integrity

The supplied rules state:

- The product must be developed during the official build window communicated for the event.
- Work completed before that window will not count toward judging.
- Participants may research, plan, interview users and discuss ideas before the window, unless event instructions state otherwise.
- Organisers may request repository access, commit history, file metadata, demonstrations or other reasonable evidence of when work was completed.
- A project substantially built before the permitted development window may be disqualified, regardless of quality.

**Project control:** Until the official start/end time and timezone are confirmed, this repository's current starter materials are classified as pre-window planning/scaffolding only. Do not represent them as event-window implementation. Do not add application functionality until the window is confirmed open.

## 2. Reuse, licences and AI assistance

The supplied rules permit existing libraries, APIs, frameworks, datasets and open-source components in accordance with their licences. Templates, boilerplate and previously created code must be clearly disclosed. Only meaningful work completed during the permitted development window will be judged. AI, no-code and low-code tools are permitted unless stated otherwise; significant tools and AI-generated components must be disclosed.

**Project control:** Maintain a truthful material register covering code, templates, synthetic fixtures, design assets, datasets, libraries, models, APIs, cloud services and AI assistance. Record licence/source, version, purpose, data handling and whether material was created before or during the official window.

## 3. Submission and individual contribution

The document identifies Round 2 submission items including a functional product/prototype/MVP, judge-accessible source repository (unless another method is approved), deployed link/installation/clear demo, architecture and technical-decision explanation, API/model/dataset/library/template/service disclosures, reasonably available user-testing evidence and each member's contribution.

The rules also state that participants must accurately report their individual contribution and that judges may inspect repository history and ask participants to explain technical decisions and demonstrate their work.

**Project control:** Keep work attributable, reviewable and explainable. The participant must understand, test and be able to defend every meaningful implementation decision.

## 5. Project's three named improvements for build evaluation

These are the three improvements Sigillum will explicitly track from implementation through final evidence. They are planned capabilities, not claims of completed functionality.

1. **Proof Ledger and principled abstention.** A factual finding must carry source/version, exact content location, quote, applicability context and a typed reason. Missing, conflicting or insufficient proof must not be presented as support.
2. **Scope-Time Truth Resolver.** Resolve facts against intended publication time and relevant product, offer, geography, audience, channel and locale. Preserve both historical review context and later changes so prior decisions can be reconstructed.
3. **Pixel-to-Claim continuity.** Text detected inside supported images enters the same structured claim and terminology workflow as document text, while retaining image-region coordinates, OCR confidence and extraction limitations.
